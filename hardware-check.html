Confirm acceleration in Firefox

Open a new tab and enter about:support. Under Graphics, examine the compositor and WebGL driver details. To review the browser setting, open Settings → General → Performance. Firefox ordinarily chooses recommended performance settings automatically. If you disable that option, you can inspect the setting named Use hardware acceleration when available.

Changing the Firefox setting may require restarting the browser. This page does not alter browser or graphics settings.
